MVP CONCEPT

A lower-friction Status experience

Explore the feed, open a Status, select a contextual suggestion, edit it, and try the send interaction. The prototype demonstrates a hypothesis—not validated user behavior.

Primary userCollege students sharing everyday moments
Core problemWanting to reply but not knowing what to say
First testOptional contextual quick replies
North StarWeekly meaningful Status interactions per active viewer

Note: This concept is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta. Sample contacts and content are fictional. The send action is simulated locally and does not send a real message.
