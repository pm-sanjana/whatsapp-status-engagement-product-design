Product rationale

Keep the first release narrow. Test whether reducing reply effort creates more meaningful conversations before expanding into discovery or AI creation tools.

P1 · MVP

Contextual quick replies

Offer a few optional prompts while viewing a Status. Users can tap, edit, or ignore them; sending still requires an explicit action.

P2 · Experiment

Interactive stickers

Explore polls and question stickers after validating demand. Preserve the poster's audience settings and keep participation controls clear.

Later

Better discovery

Investigate ways to surface relevant updates from selected contacts, but first validate how often people miss important Status posts.

Defer

AI assistance

Higher complexity and less direct evidence that creation help is the main barrier to meaningful Status engagement.

Measurement

Define success around behavior—not taps

North Star candidate: weekly meaningful Status interactions per weekly active Status viewer. Count replies, reactions, poll votes, and question responses, with safeguards against unwanted interactions.

A/B test

Compare the existing experience with quick replies among eligible users. Measure lift in meaningful interactions and actual conversations, then monitor hide, mute, report, and negative-feedback rates.

User flow

One-tap start, user-controlled send

View Status→See suggestions→Edit / ignore→Send reply

Success is a relevant response that starts a conversation—not a suggestion click by itself.

Assumptions to validate: users want help responding; suggestions feel natural; the feature increases meaningful interactions without increasing spam or notification fatigue. No actual user research or WhatsApp telemetry is represented here.
